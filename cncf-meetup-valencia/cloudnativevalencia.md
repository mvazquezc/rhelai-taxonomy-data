# Cloud Native Valencia

Cloud Native Valencia is a local community dedicated to promoting and exploring cloud-native technologies, tools, and best practices. Backed by the CNCF (Cloud Native Computing Foundation), the group brings together professionals, enthusiasts, and newcomers interested in Kubernetes, containers, DevOps, observability, and related cloud-native topics.

## Mission

Our goal is to foster collaboration, knowledge-sharing, and innovation within the cloud-native ecosystem in the Valencia region. Whether you're a seasoned cloud architect or just starting your journey into Kubernetes, you're welcome here.

## Join the Community

- [CNCF Community Portal: Cloud Native Valencia](https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-valencia/)

## Events

We organize regular events such as:

- In-person and virtual meetups
- Hands-on workshops and labs
- Lightning talks and presentations by local and international speakers
- Community collaboration days

Topics typically include:

- Kubernetes and its ecosystem
- CI/CD pipelines and GitOps
- Observability with Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry
- Serverless and microservices
- Cloud-native security
